# Competitor UX references

Reviewed 2026-09-02. These are official manufacturer sources used only to identify general display patterns. T21 Safe does not copy proprietary algorithms, validated claims, brand color systems, layouts, target ranges, or treatment language.

## Edwards Acumen HPI

Official product information describes a 0–100 parameter, repeated-update alert behavior, and a secondary screen that organizes related hemodynamic parameters. T21 Safe borrows only the patterns of a bounded research index, sustained escalation, and a deeper structured explanation. It does not reproduce HPI thresholds, predictive claims, feature derivations, or preload/afterload/contractility layout.

Source: [Edwards Lifesciences — Acumen HPI software](https://www.edwards.com/gb/healthcare-professionals/products-services/predictive-monitoring/hpi)

## Dräger SmartPilot View

Official material shows current, historical, and prospective information on a common time-oriented view and supports relating administered-drug metadata to the course of anesthesia. T21 Safe adopts a unified event/physiology timeline and retrospective replay. It intentionally excludes dose simulation, “what-if” behavior, effect-site models, and dosing optimization.

Source: [Dräger — SmartPilot View](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_ca/Products/SmartPilot-View)

## Medtronic BIS

Official BIS information pairs a processed index with raw EEG and a signal-quality indicator. Medtronic states that affected values are not displayed when signal quality is too low. T21 Safe adopts the safety pattern of showing raw signals with quality and suppressing the composite number when quality is insufficient. It makes no anesthesia-depth claim.

Source: [Medtronic — BIS monitoring system](https://www.medtronic.com/en-us/healthcare-professionals/products/patient-monitoring/brain-monitoring/brain-channel-monitoring/bis-monitoring-system.html)

## Masimo SedLine

Official SedLine material presents multiple raw EEG channels, a processed Patient State Index, artifact information, and a density spectral array. T21 Safe adopts the more general pattern that a processed number should remain inspectable beside richer signal evidence and artifact context. It does not emulate EEG spectral displays or SedLine metrics.

Source: [Masimo — SedLine brain monitoring](https://masimosafetynetalert.masimo.com/technology/brain-monitoring/sedation/)

## Medasense NOL

Official Medasense information describes a multiparameter composite of autonomic signals and a continuous numeric trend. T21 Safe adopts only the pattern of a composite research index accompanied by its contributing signal context and history. It does not reproduce NOL features, algorithms, claims, or target interpretation.

Source: [Medasense — NOL index](https://medasense.com/nol-index/)

## MDoloris ANI

Official MDoloris material describes continuous parasympathetic-tone measurement and explicitly positions ANI as an adjunct to clinical judgment rather than a standalone basis. T21 Safe adopts the general anti-overreliance principle: show the full context, quality, raw signals, and limitations around a derived index.

Source: [MDoloris — ANI Monitor V2 product sheet](https://www.mdoloris.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ANI-MONITOR-V2-Product-sheet-V05.pdf)
